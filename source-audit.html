Source review

Browse published voter guides across viewpoints.

Reviewed October 19–21, 2026. The October 21 pass adds conservative guides and local roster checks. “All” below refers to the specific published list described, not every endorsement an entity may have made.

This pass checked all 22 state November voters’ pamphlets, 189 campaign homepages and 148 linked campaign endorsement pages. Claims were reviewed in context, then matched to election-office records. A short Oregon list can be correct for a national group; a group without a public slate may have additional unverified decisions. A missing endorsement is not opposition.

Unresolved entries remain listed below so readers can distinguish a checked slate from a research gap. Read the review data.

Have you found gaps or errors in the data? Know an endorsing entity that should be added? Please create an issue on Github.
